PROJECT TIMELINE (1992–2026)

Historical project milestones and industrial plant erection legacy across 34 years.

2026 EVENT 1

Dry Mix Unit, Packing Plant & Hopper Work

2026 EVENT 2

Sizer Crusher Work Erection

Ramco Mines, Virudhunagar. Heavy structural erection of primary limestone sizer crusher, high-impact steel chutes, and discharge conveyor supports.

2025 EVENT 3

20 MW Power Plant & ACC Towers Erection

TECKSK Projects & ISGEC. Structural steel erection for 20 MW captive power plant, non-IBR pipeline supports, and Air Cooled Condenser cooling tower modules.

2025 EVENT 4

Clinker Silo Structures Work

The Ramco Cements Ltd. High-elevation heavy structural fabrication and erection for clinker silo storage facility, conveyor galleries, and transfer towers.

2023 EVENT 5

Line-3 Kiln, Coal Mill & Conveyor Gallery Erection

Complete on-site erection of rotary kiln support frames, multi-story Coal Mill processing building, and high-altitude material conveyor galleries.

2022 EVENT 6

Washing Plant Erection

The Ramco Cements Ltd. Raw material washing plant structural framework, slurry treatment platforms, and heavy mechanical pump supports.

2016–2021 EVENT 7

Coal Stacker, Reclaimer & Packing Plant Work

Continuous multi-year structural contracts including heavy automated Coal Stacker and Reclaimer rail systems, transfer stations, and continuous packing plant expansion.

2005 EVENT 8

Parryware Tiles Company Erection

Complete manufacturing plant structural steel erection, furnace bay portal frames, and automated ceramic tile handling lines.

1998 EVENT 9

Turnkey complete industrial plant structural erection, heavy extruder foundation frames, cooling bays, and finished product storage sheds.
